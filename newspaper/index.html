They Are
Getting
Married!

Amara Whitlock and Julien Bexley to wed before ninety witnesses on a hillside in Ravello; refreshment to follow, weather permitting.

RAVELLO — After four years, two cities and one poorly judged sailing holiday, the parties named above have agreed to make the arrangement permanent. The ceremony is set for the twelfth of September, at half past four in the afternoon, in the garden of the Villa Rosa.

Guests are advised that the hillside is steep, the sun is direct, and the wine is local. Comfortable shoes have been recommended by the management and ignored by the bride.

Replies are requested by the first of August. The coupon appears on the final page of this edition and may be returned without postage.

A Short History

Page Two

The Meeting, 2022

They were introduced in a queue for coffee that neither of them wanted. Witnesses report a conversation lasting eleven minutes and a subsequent, unexplained detour of four hours.

A second meeting was arranged under the pretext of returning a borrowed umbrella that had not been borrowed.

The Years Between

Two apartments, one dog of uncertain ancestry, and a running dispute regarding the correct temperature of a room. The dispute continues and will not be settled by marriage.

Fig. 2 — The dog, unimpressed.

The Question, 2025

Asked at the kitchen table on an ordinary Tuesday, without music, kneeling or a photographer concealed in the shrubbery. The answer was immediate.

The ring is her grandmother's, resized twice and worn since.

“We considered eloping. Then we considered the aunts.”

On the Question of Dress

Advisory
Garden Formal · No Black Tie

Dress as though you intend to be photographed, then walk two hundred steps in it.

Long dresses and light suits are correct. Linen is forgiven its creases. The garden is grass and gravel throughout, and heels of a narrow gauge will be lost to it.

The evening cools sharply after nine. A jacket, a shawl or a borrowed one from the couple's own supply is advised.
